The questions every couple asks

"Will it be loud enough outside?"

Outdoor spaces swallow sound. We bring speakers sized for the space, not just the guest count, so the back row hears the toast as clearly as the front.

"Will the vows actually be heard?"

Yes — mics for your officiant and your vows are standard whenever we cover the ceremony, with a wired backup on hand and everything tested before your first guest sits down.

"What if it rains?"

All our gear is weather-covered and battery/wireless options mean we're not tethered to a single power source if plans shift indoors last-minute.

"Who makes the announcements?"

Usually your planner, coordinator, or someone in the party is on the mic, and we take the cues from the booth. If you'd rather we handled the introductions and announcements, ask — we're glad to.

"How early do you set up?"

We build in buffer time and are typically fully set up and sound-checked one to two hours before your first guest arrives — quietly, out of the way.

"Multiple spaces at our venue?"

Ceremony on the lawn, cocktails on the patio, reception in the barn — we either set up a second sound zone ahead of time, or a second crew member moves and resets the system during a break while the music keeps playing. No dead air between spaces.

Planning the music

Closer to the day we'll send you a short questionnaire: your must-plays, like the first dance and the songs you'd be sad to miss, and your must-not-plays. If you have a playlist you love, send it along — it's a good way for us to hear what you're into.

On the night, we build the set around the room. We watch what's working on the floor, mix into what comes next, and work in requests where they'll keep people dancing. That means we don't play a set playlist in order, and the song you hear at eleven is chosen at eleven. If what you want is a playlist played straight through, we're probably not the right fit — and that's fine to tell us.
