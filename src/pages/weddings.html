The questions every couple asks

"Will it be loud enough outside?"

Outdoor spaces swallow sound. We bring speakers sized for the space, not just the guest count, so the back row hears the toast as clearly as the front.

"Will the vows actually be heard?"

Yes — a wireless lav on your officiant (and a backup handheld) is standard on every ceremony package, tested before your first guest sits down.

"What if it rains?"

All our gear is weather-covered and battery/wireless options mean we're not tethered to a single power source if plans shift indoors last-minute.

"Do you MC?"

On any DJ or full-service package — yes. Introductions, timeline announcements, and keeping speeches moving are part of the job, not an upsell.

"How early do you set up?"

We build in buffer time and are typically fully set up and sound-checked one to two hours before your first guest arrives — quietly, out of the way.

"Multiple spaces at our venue?"

Ceremony on the lawn, cocktails on the patio, reception in the barn — we plan audio for each space separately so nothing gets missed in the handoff.

Which package fits your wedding

A starting point — every quote is adjusted to your actual venue and timeline.

Intimate backyard or elopement

Small guest count, one space, simple setup. Fits Coastline Essentials (rental + DJ) or Backyard Basics if you just need the gear.

Barn or winery reception

Bigger room, rustic acoustics, usually a dance floor. Fits Coastline Complete — our most-booked full-service pairing.

Hotel or ballroom

Larger guest counts, house sound restrictions, sometimes multiple rooms in one night. Fits Grand Hall or Coastline Signature.

Multi-space weddings

Ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception each need their own audio zone. Fits Coastline Signature or All-In DJ + Emcee.

Outdoor ceremony, no power nearby

Needs wireless mics and battery-powered speakers. Available as an add-on to any package — flag it when you enquire.
