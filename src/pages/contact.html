How booking works

No pressure, no obligation at any step.

Step 1

Tell us about the event

The form above, or an email. Whatever you know so far is enough to start.

Step 2

We reply with a few questions

The spaces and layout, whether the venue has its own sound, the timing, and the vibe you're after.

Step 3

Options, then a quote

We send a few options for how much we cover, and a quote once the details are set.

Step 4

A signed agreement holds the date

A signed agreement and a non-refundable deposit confirm the booking. Until then, the date isn't held.
